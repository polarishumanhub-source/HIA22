A different starting point

When knowing what to do isn't enough

You may know how to organize your day, make a decision, or maintain a routine. Yet the same activity can feel manageable one day and much harder the next. Your available energy, attention, environment, responsibilities, and the demands of a particular moment all influence what feels possible.

HIM™ Core offers a structured way to explore those patterns without reducing your experience to a label or a fixed idea of what you should be able to do. You begin with a functional profile and move into educational resources and guided reflection selected for your circumstances.

The measurement architecture behind HIM™

From HIA™ to Functional Coherence™

The Human Integration Assessment™ explores patterns across interconnected dimensions of human functioning. The emerging Functional Coherence Index™ (FCI™) is designed to bring those patterns into an integrated picture, rather than treating each domain as an isolated score.

HIA™ · Human Integration Assessment

Explore your functional profile across nervous system regulation, emotional integration, identity alignment, relational coherence, and somatic awareness. Learn how these dimensions may influence one another in daily life.

Explore HIA™ ↗

FCI™ · Functional Coherence Index

FCI™ is the developing integrative index within the HIA™ measurement framework. It examines modeled relationships across functional domains, including capacity, synchrony, reciprocity, and harmony. It is not a clinical diagnosis or a measure of personal worth.

Who this is for

Is HIM™ Core Right for You?

HIM™ Core is for adults curious about how they function in everyday life and interested in exploring practical educational approaches without expecting a one-size-fits-all answer.

  • You want to better understand your everyday functional patterns.
  • You've tried strategies that haven't always fit your circumstances.
  • You're curious about how context and available resources influence what you can do.
  • You value individualized educational guidance and reflection.
  • You're willing to observe, experiment, and adjust without expecting instant results.

No diagnosis or previous experience is required. HIM™ Core is educational and developmental; it does not provide psychotherapy, diagnosis, or medical treatment.

Good questions deserve clear answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to read the answer.

What exactly is HIM™ Core Personal Pathway?

HIM™ Core is a personalized educational experience that combines an HIA™ Functional Assessment, selected learning resources, and private FIT educational review sessions to explore your everyday functional patterns and practical possibilities.

Do I need previous experience or a diagnosis?

No. HIM™ Core is designed for adults interested in personal understanding and development. You do not need a diagnosis or prior knowledge of the methodology.

What is the HIA™ Functional Assessment?

It is an educational assessment that offers an individualized Functional Profile as a starting point for reflection and learning. It is not a clinical evaluation, medical diagnosis, or permanent classification of your abilities.

What happens during private FIT sessions?

FIT sessions are individual 30-minute educational review meetings with Claudia Zamora. They offer time to discuss your profile, learning observations, questions, and potential adjustments. They are not psychotherapy or medical consultations.

How do I access my learning materials?

Educational materials are provided digitally through the program's learning environment. Resources are selected according to your starting profile and chosen program level. Access instructions are provided as part of enrollment.

What is the difference between the three programs?

Foundations lasts two weeks and includes one FIT session. Functional Pathway lasts four weeks, expands the resources, and includes two FIT sessions in total. Integrated lasts eight weeks, includes four FIT sessions in total, and incorporates a closing HIA™ reassessment.

Can I continue to a higher level later?

Yes, the pathways are organized progressively. If you want to continue after Foundations or Functional Pathway, you can inquire about enrollment in the next level. Any upgrade arrangements and pricing should be confirmed before you proceed.

Is HIM™ Core a certification program?

No. HIM™ Core Personal Pathway is an individual educational and developmental experience. Professional training and certification offerings, if available, are separate.

Is HIM™ therapy or medical treatment?

No. HIM™ Core does not provide psychotherapy, medical treatment, clinical diagnosis, or guaranteed health outcomes. It offers structured educational learning, practical exploration, and guided reflection.
